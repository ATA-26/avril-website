Two ways to sell

Choose the path that pays you the most

Every home and every seller is different. That's why we bring you two real options instead of one take-it-or-leave-it number.

Option one

Straight cash

The fastest, most certain way to sell. We buy your house directly with our own funds — no banks, no financing contingencies, no waiting on a buyer. It's the right call when you value speed and certainty above everything else.

  • Sold completely as-is — no repairs, cleaning, or staging
  • Close in as little as seven days, or whenever suits you
  • No fees, no commissions, no financing that can fall through
Best when…

You need it done fast

Foreclosure timelines, a quick relocation, or you simply want the certainty of a guaranteed sale and a check in hand.

Option two

Novation

A way to reach for the home's full market value without doing the work yourself. Under a novation agreement, we take responsibility for lightly preparing and marketing the property, sell it at retail, and pass more of the proceeds to you — while you stay hands-off the entire time.

  • Typically nets you more than a straight cash offer
  • We coordinate the prep and marketing — you don't lift a finger
  • No agent commissions coming out of your pocket
Often pays more Best when…

You have a little time

The home is in decent shape or a strong area, and getting the highest possible price matters more than the fastest possible close.
