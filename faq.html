Questions, answered

Everything you might want to know before you request an offer. Don't see yours? Just call — we're happy to help.

Is my offer really no-obligation?

Completely. We'll present a fair offer and answer every question you have. You only move forward if you're happy with it — there's no cost and no pressure to accept.

What's the difference between a cash offer and novation?

With a cash offer, we buy your house directly for speed and certainty — usually the fastest way to close. With novation, we take on the property under agreement, handle light prep and marketing, and sell it closer to full market value, then pass more of the proceeds to you. Novation often nets you more; cash is faster. We'll compare both for your home.

Do I need to make repairs or clean up first?

No. We buy houses as-is. Leave what you don't want, skip the repairs, and don't worry about staging or showings. We handle the condition of the property.

Are there any fees or commissions?

No agent commissions and no service fees from us. A title company handles closing to keep everything clean and legitimate. We'll always be upfront about the numbers before you sign anything.

How fast can you close?

On a cash purchase, often in as little as seven days. If you need more time to move or plan, that's no problem — you choose the closing date that works for you.

Where do you buy houses?

We're based in Seattle, Washington and buy properties nationwide. Wherever your house is located, reach out and we'll let you know how we can help.

What kinds of situations do you work with?

All kinds — inherited or probate properties, homes that need major repairs, tired-landlord rentals, divorce, pre-foreclosure, relocation, downsizing, and more. Most of the people we help are not facing foreclosure; they simply want a fast, easy sale.

How do I get started?

Fill out the form on our contact page or call (425) 675-9964. Share the address and a little about your situation, and we'll follow up within 24 hours.

Still have a question?

Call us and get a straight answer — no pressure, no obligation.
