Two ways to sell

Most buyers only pay cash. We give you a choice.

Depending on your home and your goals, one of these will put the most money in your pocket. We'll walk you through both — you decide.

The fast, certain route

Straight cash

We buy your house outright with our own funds. Simple, quick, and guaranteed — ideal when speed and certainty matter most.

  • Sold as-is — no repairs or cleanup
  • Close on your date, in as little as a week
  • No financing to fall through
Often pays more The top-dollar route

Novation

We take on the property under agreement, handle the light prep and marketing, and sell it for its full retail value — then hand you a bigger check. You still skip the hassle.

  • Get closer to full market value
  • We manage the work — you stay hands-off
  • No agent commissions coming out of your pocket

Not sure which fits? Call us and we'll compare both for your property — free.
