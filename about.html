Avril Solutions is a real estate solutions company based in Seattle, Washington, helping homeowners across the country sell on their terms.

Who we are

A different kind of home buyer

We're not real estate agents, and we're not here to list your house. We're direct buyers. That means no commissions, no open houses, and no waiting months for the right offer to appear.

What sets us apart is choice. Most cash buyers hand you one number and hope you take it. We look at your property two ways — a straight cash purchase for speed and certainty, or a novation to reach for full market value — and we show you both, honestly, so you can pick what puts the most money in your pocket.

We've built Avril Solutions on a simple idea: selling a house shouldn't add stress to your life. Whether you're dealing with an inherited property, a tough tenant, a looming deadline, or you just want out cleanly, our job is to make it easy and treat you with respect — even if you decide not to sell to us.
